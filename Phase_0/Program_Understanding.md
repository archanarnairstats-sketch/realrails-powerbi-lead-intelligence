Program
Real Rails – Power BI: Lead Management & Conversion Intelligence

1. Objective

The objective of this internship is to build a Power BI solution for Lead Management & Conversion Intelligence.

The project uses Academic CRM data to understand how leads are generated, managed, followed up, converted into admissions, and eventually enrolled.

The final Power BI solution should help management understand:

 Lead volume
 Lead source performance
 Lead conversion
 Conversion funnel and drop-off
 Counsellor performance
 Lead ageing
 Follow-up issues
 Leads requiring attention

2. Internship Phases

The internship is divided into four phases:

### Phase 0 — Power BI Environment, Program & Domain Foundation

This phase focuses on setting up Power BI, understanding the internship objective, understanding the Academic CRM domain, and completing a small Power BI setup exercise.

### Phase 1 — Power Query & Analytical Data Model

This phase focuses on loading and cleaning the CRM source data, understanding table grain, identifying keys and relationships, profiling data quality, and creating a validated analytical model.

### Phase 2 — DAX & Lead Intelligence

This phase focuses on creating and validating DAX measures for lead volume, admissions, enrollments, conversion, follow-ups, ageing, counsellor performance, and operational attention.

### Phase 3 — Power BI Lead Management Dashboard

This phase focuses on converting the validated data model and DAX measures into a professional, interactive Power BI dashboard.

3. What I Am Expected to Learn

Through this internship, I am expected to learn how to:

 Understand a business problem before building a dashboard.
 Understand the structure and grain of CRM data.
 Use Power Query to inspect and transform data.
 Identify primary keys, foreign keys, and relationships.
 Build a suitable analytical data model.
 Create DAX measures for business analysis.
 Validate measures and calculations.
 Design professional Power BI report pages.
 Use slicers, cross-filtering, drill-through, tooltips, and conditional formatting.
 Present business information through clear visualisations.
 Validate that the dashboard answers the required business questions.

4. Expected Final Outcome

The final outcome of the internship is a professional Power BI Lead Management & Conversion Intelligence dashboard.

The dashboard should allow management or operations users to understand:

 What is happening with the leads.
 Which lead sources are performing well.
 Where leads are dropping out of the conversion process.
 How counsellors are performing.
 Which leads are becoming stale or unattended.
 Which follow-ups require attention.
 What operational actions should be considered next.

The dashboard should be based on the validated data model and DAX measures rather than hardcoded sample values.

5. How AI Will Be Used

AI will be used as a mentor throughout the internship.

AI can help me:

 Understand Power BI concepts.
 Understand the Academic CRM business domain.
 Understand table structures and relationships.
 Review Power Query transformations.
 Explain DAX concepts and measures.
 Troubleshoot errors.
 Validate calculations.
 Review dashboard design.
 Identify possible corrections.

AI will not replace my own implementation work. I will build the Power BI solution myself and use AI to understand, implement, troubleshoot, and validate my work.

The internship follows a mentor-based approach where AI supports my learning and implementation rather than completing the internship for me.
